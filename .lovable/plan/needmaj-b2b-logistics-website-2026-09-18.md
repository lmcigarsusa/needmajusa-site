# Needmaj B2B logistics website

## Goal
Build a clean, modern, mobile-first website that makes Needmaj’s full freight-handling process understandable within seconds and makes calling or requesting a quote effortless.

## What I’ll build
- A compact header with the existing Needmaj logo, simple navigation, direct phone access, and a prominent quote button.
- A strong warehouse image opening with one clear message, the core service list, and Call Now / Get a Quote actions.
- Clear sections for business benefits, the four-step freight process, cross-docking, all eight services, customer types, storage, and South Florida port logistics.
- A short, validated quote form plus prominent contact information for Jacob (754-357-7323) and Michel (561-866-4404).
- A focused footer with the address, email, phone contacts, and service-area language.

## Visual direction
- Deep navy conveys trust, stability, and secure handling.
- Clear blue supports reliability and operational clarity.
- Restrained amber highlights only high-intent actions and process cues.
- White and cool gray keep the page clean, readable, and premium.
- Strong industrial typography, realistic warehouse imagery, crisp geometry, and limited motion.

## Technical details
- Responsive single-page experience with working section navigation and mobile menu.
- Click-to-call and email links, accessible controls, form validation, and a clear submission success state.
- Search-focused page metadata and structured local business information using only provided facts.
- Optimized local imagery, semantic design tokens, reduced-motion support, and no invented claims or statistics.
